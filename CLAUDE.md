# Behavioral Interview Prep

## What This Is
FAANG/MAANG behavioral interview preparation — 13 markdown docs covering STAR method, Amazon Leadership Principles (all 16), Google Googleyness, company values (Meta/Apple/Netflix/Microsoft), 7 question categories with STAR templates, level calibration guide, and story bank template.

## Structure
- 01: STAR method framework (STAR, STAR-L, STAR-I)
- 02: Amazon Leadership Principles (16 LPs with questions)
- 03: Google Googleyness (9 traits)
- 04: Company values (Meta, Apple, Netflix, Microsoft)
- 05-11: Question categories with STAR templates, examples, red flags
- 12: Senior vs Staff calibration guide
- 13: Story bank template (fill in your own stories)

## How to Use
1. Read 01 (STAR method) first
2. Read your target company's values (02-04)
3. Read category files (05-11) for question-specific templates
4. Fill in 13 (Story Bank) with your 8-10 personal stories
5. Use 12 to calibrate stories to your target level
