# Leadership and Initiative -- "Tell me about a time you led..."

> At Senior/Staff level, EVERY story should demonstrate leadership — even in failure or conflict stories.

## Amazon LPs: Ownership, Bias for Action, Think Big, Hire and Develop the Best
## Google: Bias Toward Action, Challenging Status Quo

---

## Top Questions (2024-2025)

1. Tell me about a time you led a team through a difficult situation.
2. Describe a situation where you took initiative without being asked.
3. What project are you most proud of leading, and why?
4. Tell me about a time you mentored or helped develop someone junior.
5. Describe a time you had to rally a team around an unpopular decision.
6. Tell me about a time you identified a problem nobody else saw and drove the fix.
7. Describe a time you created scope from nothing (identified and championed work not on the roadmap).

---

## STAR Template

### Situation
*"On [team/project], I noticed that [problem nobody else was addressing]. This was impacting [metric/team/customers]."*

### Task
*"Nobody had been assigned to fix this. I took it upon myself to [investigate/propose/lead] because [why it mattered]."*

### Action
1. **Identified the opportunity/problem** — "I analyzed [data/logs/user feedback] and found..."
2. **Proposed a solution** — "I wrote a one-pager proposing [approach] and shared it with [stakeholders]..."
3. **Built buy-in** — "I presented to [who] and addressed their concerns about [risk/cost/timeline]..."
4. **Led the execution** — "I organized the team, broke the work into [milestones], and personally handled [hardest part]..."
5. **Delivered and measured** — "We shipped [what] which improved [metric] by [amount]..."

### Result
*"The initiative [reduced incidents by X% / saved Y hours per week / generated $Z revenue]. It was later adopted as a standard practice across [scope]."*

## Leadership at Different Levels

### Mid-Level Leadership
- Leading a feature within your team
- Mentoring a new hire or intern
- Proposing a process improvement
- Taking ownership of a bug or incident

### Senior Leadership
- Leading a cross-team project
- Defining technical direction for a component
- Mentoring multiple junior engineers
- Setting technical standards for your team

### Staff+ Leadership
- Identifying and championing org-wide initiatives
- Influencing VP/Director-level decisions
- Creating a technical vision that others rally around
- Being a force multiplier — making 10 engineers more effective
- Creating scope from nothing ("I identified this problem that wasn't on anyone's roadmap")

## Mentorship Stories (Hire and Develop the Best)

### STAR Template for Mentorship:
- **S:** "A junior engineer on my team was struggling with [specific skill/situation]..."
- **T:** "I decided to invest time in helping them because [why]..."
- **A:** "I set up weekly 1:1s focused on [topic]. I paired with them on [project]. I gave specific, actionable feedback on [what]..."
- **R:** "Within [timeframe], they were able to [specific achievement]. They later [got promoted / led a project / became the go-to person for X]..."

---

## Red Flags

- Claiming credit for the team's work ("I did everything")
- No delegation — did you just do it all yourself?
- "I was told to lead this" — show initiative, not assignment
- Only leading easy projects — show you led through difficulty
