## The Top 10 Most-Asked Questions

Every one of these has a full 2-minute sample STAR answer in [`14-Top-10-Questions-Samples.md`](14-Top-10-Questions-Samples.md) and the [Top-10 canvas](canvas/questions/top-10-samples.html).

| # | Question | Frequency | Primary LP / Trait |
|---|----------|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | Tell me about a time you had a conflict with a coworker | ~90% | Earn Trust · Have Backbone |
| 2 | Tell me about a time you failed. What did you learn? | ~100% | Insist on Standards · Humility |
| 3 | Tell me about your greatest professional accomplishment | ~70% | Deliver Results · Think Big |
| 4 | Describe a decision you made with incomplete information | ~65% | Bias for Action · Ambiguity |
| 5 | Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager | ~75% | Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit |
| 6 | Tell me about a time you took ownership outside your area | ~60% | Ownership · Challenging Status Quo |
| 7 | Tell me about a time you went above and beyond for a customer | ~70% | Customer Obsession · Users First |
| 8 | Tell me about a time you received critical feedback | ~60% | Humility · Feedback |
| 9 | Tell me about a time you led a team through a difficult situation | ~55% | Ownership · Deliver Results |
| 10 | Tell me about a time you had to influence without authority | ~50% | Earn Trust · Collaborative |

**Preparation math:** If you can nail these 10 questions with 8-10 versatile stories, you're covered for ~80% of what you'll be asked.

## The Golden Rules

1. **Always use "I", not "we"** — they want YOUR contribution (2:1 "I":we ratio+)
2. **Quantify results** — "improved latency by 40%" not "made it faster"
3. **Prepare 8-10 stories** — each should cover 2-3 categories
4. **2 minutes per answer** — concise, not rambling (use the [STAR builder](canvas/frameworks/star-method.html) to check)
5. **End with learnings** — especially for failure stories (STAR-L for Google)
6. **Match your level** — Staff stories need org-wide impact, not just team-level
7. **Study the Top 10 samples** — 10 great answers cover ~80% of what you'll be asked

## Practice Cadence (4 weeks)

| Week | Activity |
|------|----------|
| **Week 1** | Read [STAR method](01-STAR-Method.md), your target company's [values](02-Amazon-Leadership-Principles.md), and the [Top 10 samples](14-Top-10-Questions-Samples.md). Draft your 8-10 stories in the [Story Bank](canvas/frameworks/story-bank.html). |
| **Week 2** | Write full STAR answers for the [Top 10 questions](14-Top-10-Questions-Samples.md) using your real stories. Check pacing with the [STAR builder](canvas/frameworks/star-method.html). |
| **Week 3** | Practice with a peer. Record yourself. Refine using the [red-flag lists](05-Conflict-Resolution.md) in each category file. |
| **Week 4** | Practice adapting a single story to multiple questions. Calibrate to your target level ([P-01 canvas](canvas/prep/level-calibration.html)). |
