# Story Bank Template -- Build Your 8-10 Go-To Stories

Prepare **8-10 versatile stories** from your career. Each story should cover **2-3 categories** so you can adapt to any question.

---

## Instructions

1. Copy this template for each story
2. Fill in the STAR sections
3. Tag which categories and LPs each story covers
4. Practice each story to **under 2 minutes**
5. Practice adapting the same story to different questions

---

## Story Ideas (pull from your career)

Think about times you:
- Shipped something you're proud of
- Fixed a critical production incident
- Disagreed with your manager or peer
- Failed and recovered
- Mentored someone
- Worked across teams
- Made a difficult technical decision
- Dealt with unclear requirements
- Improved a process or system nobody asked you to fix
- Advocated for the user/customer
