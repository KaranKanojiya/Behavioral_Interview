Question Categories — 8 topics · the top 60+ questions
Q-00 · start here
Top 10 with Full STAR Samples
The 10 questions that cover ~80% of what you'll actually be asked. Ranked by frequency (2024-2025). Each includes what interviewers listen for, LP mapping, a full 2-minute STAR sample answer, and common follow-ups. Study the structure, adapt with your story.
Q-01 · #1 asked
Conflict Resolution
"Tell me about a disagreement..." — the #1 most-asked behavioral question. Listen-first STAR template, respect + data pattern, disagree-and-commit. Amazon: Earn Trust, Have Backbone.
Q-02 · universal
Failure & Learning
"Tell me about a time you failed..." Every FAANG asks this. Own it, fix it, systemic prevention. Google's Intellectual Humility. Avoid humble-brag failures.
Q-03 · senior+
Leadership & Initiative
"Tell me about a time you led..." At Senior/Staff, every story shows leadership. Create-scope-from-nothing pattern. Amazon: Ownership, Bias for Action, Hire & Develop.
Q-04 · separates staff
Ambiguity & Decisions
"Incomplete information..." One-way vs two-way doors, 70% rule, reversible decisions. This category separates Senior from Staff. Google's Comfort with Ambiguity.
Q-05 · Amazon #1
Customer Obsession
"Above and beyond..." Amazon's #1 LP. Push-back-on-PM stories. Backend engineers: your "customer" is internal devs, on-call, downstream teams. Google's Putting Users First.
Q-06 · greatest hit
Impact & Results
"Biggest accomplishment..." Your killer story with heavy metrics. Latency, revenue, adoption, headcount saved. Meta's Focus on Impact. Amazon: Deliver Results.
Q-07 · cross-functional
Teamwork & Collaboration
"Cross-functional..." Adapt communication style, SBI feedback model, help-a-struggling-teammate pattern. Google's Collaborative Nature. Amazon: Earn Trust, Hire & Develop.
