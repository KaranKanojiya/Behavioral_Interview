# Teamwork and Collaboration -- "Tell me about a cross-functional project..."

## Amazon LPs: Earn Trust, Hire and Develop the Best
## Google: Collaborative Nature, Empathy

---

## Top Questions (2024-2025)

1. Tell me about a time you worked effectively in a cross-functional team.
2. Describe a situation where you adapted your communication style for a different audience.
3. Give an example of when you helped a struggling teammate.
4. Tell me about a time you had to build consensus across teams with competing priorities.
5. Describe a time you onboarded onto a new team or codebase. How did you ramp up?
6. Tell me about a time you gave difficult feedback to a peer.

---

## STAR Template

### Situation
*"I was working on [project] that required collaboration between [engineering, PM, design, data science, etc.]. The challenge was [competing priorities / different technical opinions / communication gaps]."*

### Task
*"I needed to [align the teams / bridge the communication gap / deliver a cross-team deliverable]."*

### Action
1. **Understood each team's perspective** — "I scheduled 1:1s with [each stakeholder] to understand their constraints and priorities..."
2. **Found common ground** — "I identified that we all agreed on [shared goal] even though we disagreed on [approach]..."
3. **Adapted communication** — "I translated technical concepts for the PM using [analogies/diagrams] and business impact for engineering using [metrics]..."
4. **Created alignment mechanisms** — "I set up a weekly sync with shared [doc/dashboard/Slack channel] to track progress..."
5. **Delivered together** — "I coordinated the integration across teams, ensuring [no blocking dependencies]..."

### Result
*"We shipped [deliverable] on time. The cross-team collaboration model I set up was later adopted for [other projects]."*

## Helping a Struggling Teammate

### Template:
- **S:** "I noticed [teammate] was struggling with [specific challenge — new codebase, complex feature, personal circumstances]..."
- **T:** "Nobody had explicitly asked me to help, but I decided to step in because [impact on team/project if they continued struggling]..."
- **A:** "I [paired with them / set up 1:1 sessions / broke the problem into smaller pieces / shared relevant resources]. Specifically, I..."
- **R:** "Within [timeframe], they were able to [deliver the feature / gain confidence / become independent]. They later [specific outcome]."

**Key:** Show it was YOUR initiative, not assigned by a manager.

---

## Giving Difficult Feedback

### Template:
- **S:** "A peer's [code quality / approach / behavior] was impacting [team/project/quality]..."
- **A:** Describe the SBI model:
  - **S**ituation: "In yesterday's code review..."
  - **B**ehavior: "I noticed the error handling was missing for the API calls..."
  - **I**mpact: "This could cause silent failures in production..."
- **R:** "They appreciated the feedback and [fixed the issue / changed their approach]. Our code quality improved."

**Key:** Show you gave feedback with empathy, not criticism.

---

## Red Flags

- "I did most of the work" — sounds like a poor collaborator
- "The other team was difficult" — blame-shifting
- "My manager handled the alignment" — show YOU drove collaboration
- No mention of adapting communication style — one-size-fits-all communicator
